# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want sne to be a project where anyone can take part without being harassed, whatever their age,
body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race,
religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are volunteers, and many are not native English speakers.
- Assume good intentions, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not about the person.
- Own your mistakes, say sorry when it is due, and learn from them.

## Behaviour that is not accepted

- Insults, personal attacks and demeaning comments.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

In every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, code review and any other place where
someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks these rules, report it to the maintainers. Use the
**Report content** option that GitHub shows on every comment, issue and pull request, or contact a
member of the [sne-sh organization](https://github.com/sne-sh) privately.

Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers will review each one and respond in the way they judge
right for the situation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that
break this Code of Conduct. Depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is, they may give
a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban from the project.
